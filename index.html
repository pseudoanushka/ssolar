FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about solar energy

The cost depends on system size and your energy needs. Residential systems (3-5 kW) typically range from ₹1.5-3 lakhs. We offer flexible financing options, government subsidies, and EMI plans to make solar affordable. Contact us for a personalized quote.

The Central Government offers subsidies up to 40% for residential rooftop solar systems under the PM Surya Ghar scheme. For systems up to 3 kW, you can receive ₹18,000/kW subsidy, and for 3-10 kW systems, ₹9,000/kW for additional capacity. We handle all subsidy applications.

Typical residential installations take 2-3 days for physical mounting and electrical work. The complete process from site survey to grid connection takes 2-4 weeks, depending on permits and utility approvals. We coordinate everything for you.

Solar panels come with 25-year performance warranty (minimum 80% efficiency after 25 years). Inverters have 5-10 year warranties. We also provide 5-year comprehensive installation warranty covering workmanship and system operation.

Yes, solar panels generate power even on cloudy days, though at reduced capacity (15-30% of peak output). With net-metering, excess energy produced during sunny days is credited back, balancing out lower production periods. Battery backup is also available for continuous power.

Net-metering allows you to export excess solar power to the grid and receive credits on your electricity bill. When your panels produce more than you consume, the surplus is fed back to the grid. At night or during low production, you draw from the grid using these credits. It's available through MSEDCL in Maharashtra.

Solar panels require minimal maintenance. Clean them 2-3 times per year to remove dust and debris. Check connections and inverter performance quarterly. We offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC) that include cleaning, inspection, and performance monitoring to ensure optimal output.

Yes, homes with solar installations typically see a 3-4% increase in property value. Solar systems are viewed as upgrades similar to renovated kitchens or finished basements. Buyers are willing to pay a premium for homes with lower operating costs and energy independence.
